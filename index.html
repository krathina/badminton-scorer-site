Badminton Scorer

A clean, fast match tracker for badminton players.

Features

Standard badminton scoring (first to 21, best of 3 games), deuce and match point alerts, undo, editable player names, serve indicator, match history with per-game score breakdowns, and share results with friends. Works entirely offline.
