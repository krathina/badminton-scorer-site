Frequently Asked Questions

How does scoring work?

Badminton Scorer follows standard BWF (Badminton World Federation) rules. A game is won by the first player to reach 21 points with at least a 2-point lead. If the score reaches 29-all, the first player to reach 30 wins. A match is best of 3 games.

What do the service court indicators mean?

The app shows "Right" or "Left" next to the serve icon, indicating which service court the server should serve from. Per BWF rules, serve from the right court when your score is even (0, 2, 4...) and the left court when your score is odd (1, 3, 5...).

When should we switch ends?

The app automatically prompts you to switch ends after each game and at 11 points in the deciding (third) game. This follows standard BWF rules.

How do I set player names?

The app asks for player names when you start a new match. You can also tap on a player's name during a match to edit it.

How do I undo a point?

Tap the "Undo" button at the bottom of the screen to remove the last point scored. You can undo multiple times to walk back through the entire rally history.

How do I delete a match from history?

In the Match History screen, swipe a match card to the left to delete it. Deleted matches cannot be recovered.

Where is my data stored?

All match history is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to the cloud. Each device has its own separate database, so matches played on one device will not appear on another.

Can I recover deleted data?

If you uninstall the app, all match history is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. We recommend using the Share feature to save important match results before uninstalling.

Is the app accessible?

Yes. The scoring panels include accessibility labels that work with VoiceOver (iOS) and TalkBack (Android), describing the player name, score, and serving status.

Does the screen stay awake during a match?

Yes. The screen stays on while a match is in progress so you don't have to unlock your phone between rallies. It returns to normal when the match ends or you start a new match.
