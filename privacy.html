Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Overview

Badminton Scorer is designed with privacy in mind. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your match data stays on your device.

Data Collection

Badminton Scorer does not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, login, or any form of registration to use.

Data Storage

Match history and scores are stored locally on your device in a SQLite database. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all stored data.

Analytics and Tracking

Badminton Scorer does not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, or tracking technologies. No usage data is collected or transmitted.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party services. The share feature uses your device's built-in share sheet and only shares content you explicitly choose to share.

Advertising

Badminton Scorer does not display advertisements and does not share any data with advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the App Store or Google Play Store review system, or open an issue on our GitHub page.
